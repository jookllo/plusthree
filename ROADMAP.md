# Plusthree build roadmap

Updated 5 November 2026. See [README.md](README.md) for the detailed handoff and acceptance criteria, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for contributor instructions.

Stack: Laravel 12, MySQL, Blade and Tailwind/Vite with Filament 5.11 for the `/mdosi` panel.

1. **Foundation — complete for local development.** Laravel, isolated development/test MySQL databases, build tooling and baseline checks.
2. **Content schema — implemented 31 October 2026; job editorial fields added 5 November 2026.** Companies, `job_listings` (not the queue `jobs` table), articles, shared taxonomies, `users.is_admin`, publication/deadline scopes, factories and tests; details in [SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md). The public site and `/mdosi` now read these tables.
3. **Publishing admin — core implemented 31 October 2026; rich text and preview added 1 November 2026; job/article parity 5 November 2026.** Filament 5.11 at `/mdosi` (not the default `/admin`), admin-only access, CRUD for companies, jobs, articles, categories and tags, rich-text editors with in-body images, featured images with required alt text, SEO overrides, draft previews and the describe-images action for both articles and job listings. Outstanding: the describe-images check on the create screen, a scheduled image prune, 2FA and user management.
4. **Public design — preview implemented; finish remains.** Brand colours, provisional Africa logo, search-led layout, ruled job rows, editorial sidebar, five-link inline header and responsive checks. Real imagery and final logo are outstanding.
5. **Job discovery — implemented 1 November 2026; pagination, urgency ordering and closing-soon emphasis 5 November 2026.** `/jobs` searches published, open listings by keyword and contract type with shareable URLs and numbered pagination; `/jobs/{slug}` shows details and a safe apply destination. Every list — including the paginated homepage — puts roles closing within seven days first (soonest deadline at the top) and marks them with a red “Closes today/tomorrow” chip on a tinted, red-ruled row; `?closing=soon` filters to them. Expired, draft and future roles are excluded by query; expired and archived roles keep a readable page with the closed notice and no `JobPosting` data. Part-time is an employment type. The location selector stays removed. Still missing: a sector filter, and a redirect policy for long-closed roles.
6. **Blog — implemented 1 November 2026.** `/blog` (newest article as lead), `/blog/{slug}` with related reading, `/blog/category/{slug}`, and homepage teasers all read published articles. Article featured images upload through `/mdosi` with required alt text; articles without one use a generated hero panel. Rich-text bodies, tag pages and article search remain.
7. **SEO — metadata, sitemap and structured data implemented 1 November 2026; closed-role schema gating 5 November 2026.** Canonicals, Open Graph, `sitemap.xml`, generated `robots.txt`, `Article`/`JobPosting`/`WebSite` JSON-LD and an opt-in `SEO_INDEXABLE` policy. Closed and archived roles no longer emit `JobPosting`. Outstanding: Search Console, slug redirects and the 301-after-grace-period redirect.
8. **Verification — 123 tests / 489 assertions passing on 5 November 2026.** Homepage/search/database/visibility/SEO coverage, plus responsive browser checks at 320–1280px. Production readiness checks, accessibility audit and performance measurement remain.
9. **Deployment — live 1 November 2026.** Dokploy application and MySQL service, production environment, Cloudflare domain with Let's Encrypt HTTPS, and a named `plusthree-uploads` volume at `/var/www/html/storage/app/public`. Still required: production migrations for the newer columns, real content, backups with a restore drill, monitoring, and Search Console submission.

Initial release: public browsing without accounts; admin-managed jobs/articles; applications through employer links/email. Applicant tracking, CV uploads, payments and employer self-service are deferred.

Do not run migrations or destructive tests on another application's schema. Preserve the existing shared MySQL container and unrelated data. No deployment or complete CMS is implied by the local preview.
